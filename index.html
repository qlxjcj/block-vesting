Escrow GEN that vests over a block range

GEN is escrowed in wei (18 decimals). Time is an Ethereum block height, never a clock: the two providers below can disagree by blocks, so the contract always counts from the laggard. Nothing vests at the first block; everything has vested at the last.

Funds move only when someone releases or cancels.
